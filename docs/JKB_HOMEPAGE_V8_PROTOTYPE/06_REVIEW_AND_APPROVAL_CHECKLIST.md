### 2. Evaluation Criteria Matrix

Please review the prototype on both **Desktop (1440px / Laptop)** and **Mobile (390px / Smartphone)** using the questions below:

#### A. Creative Impact & Art Direction
- [ ] **First 5 Seconds:** Does a first-time visitor immediately grasp that this event is a fusion of professional kickboxing championship AND live music festival?
- [ ] **Originality:** Does the page feel like an original, premium editorial festival website rather than a generic SaaS dashboard or AI-generated template?
- [ ] **Cultural Fit:** Does the narrative (*"DI BALIK KALEM — JOGJA PUNYA NYALI"*) feel authentically rooted in Yogyakarta's youth and athletic culture?

#### B. Content Structure & Section Balance
- [ ] **Section 01 (Immersive Hero):** Is the headline bold and impactful without feeling cluttered? Are the date (`12–14 Desember 2026`) and venue (`GOR UNY`) immediately obvious?
- [ ] **Section 02 (The Ring / The Stage):** Does the 7-to-5 asymmetrical layout create dynamic contrast between the sport tournament and the festival stage?
- [ ] **Section 03 (Plan Your Visit):** Does the transition to **Warm Ivory (`#F5F3EE`)** feel like a sophisticated editorial shift that improves reading comfort, or does it feel disconnected?
- [ ] **Section 04 (Beyond The Festival):** Are City Pride, Creator Hub, and Merchandise presented compactly without feeling like bloated mini-landing pages?

#### C. Mobile Experience & Usability
- [ ] **Headline Legibility:** Does *"JOGJA PUNYA NYALI."* fit neatly without horizontal clipping or awkward wrapping on small screens (320px/390px)?
- [ ] **Touch Ergonomics:** Are primary CTA buttons (*"INFO TIKET & AKSES"*, *"LIHAT JADWAL 3 HARI"*) effortless to tap with one thumb?
- [ ] **Mobile Navigation:** Is the slide-down menu drawer intuitive, clean, and fast to close?

#### D. Content Truthfulness & Commercial Integrity
- [ ] **Zero Fabricated Content:** Are all dates, athlete counts (80 atlet), and tournament divisions aligned with official facts?
- [ ] **Safe Commercial Gates:** Are pending ticketing (RAWOOH) and streaming (PPV) features represented transparently without fake payment links or unapproved promises?
