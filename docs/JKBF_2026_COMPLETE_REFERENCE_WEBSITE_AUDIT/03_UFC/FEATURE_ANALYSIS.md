# UFC — feature analysis

**Observed URLs:** `https://www.ufc.com/`; `https://www.ufc.com/event/ufc-333`.

## Homepage and event page
The homepage is editorial-first: a large lead story, numbered Top Stories, a next-event countdown, video/editorial cards and a substantial footer. UFC 333 structures the event into Main Card, Prelims and Early Prelims. Each bout includes division, athlete links, flags, and odds; separate blocks handle start times, how to watch, tickets, app promotion and social sharing.

## Strengths
- Excellent information hierarchy for audiences who need events, athletes, results, news and watch actions.
- Clear three-tier card structure.
- Localised times and explicit “how to watch” reduce friction.
- Athlete links turn a fight card into a discoverable directory.

## Weaknesses / adaptation risk
- Editorial volume can overwhelm a smaller festival website.
- Betting odds are unsuitable for JKBF unless explicitly approved and legally reviewed.

## JKBF adaptation
Borrow the card hierarchy and “how to attend/watch” block, but keep JKBF focused on a three-day live festival. Priority P0 for fight-card clarity, P1 for editorial modules, complexity Medium.
