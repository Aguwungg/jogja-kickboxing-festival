# Patterns to avoid

- Copying BYON’s event-poster treatment or UFC’s institutional editorial look.
- Repeating full-width red sections until every section has the same visual weight.
- Generic SaaS cards for fighters, tickets or festival pillars.
- Too many competing CTA buttons.
- Bracket or schedule density without filters, day selectors or clear hierarchy.
- Large display type without supporting metadata.
- Artificial or poorly cropped athlete imagery.
- Overbuilt mega menus for a first-edition festival.
- Treating merchandise as a detached store instead of part of the festival story.
- Introducing odds, PPV or login requirements without a real business need.
- Changing existing authentication, payment, database, Prisma schema, RBAC, APIs or admin workflows during a visual redesign.
