# GLORY Kickboxing — feature analysis

**Observed URLs:** `https://glorykickboxing.com/`; `https://glorykickboxing.com/events/collision-10`.

## Event detail
Collision 10 combines event title, heavyweight title context, 8-man welterweight grand prix, date, venue, countdown, ticket link, sponsor marks, a fight-card section, and round tabs for quarterfinals, semifinals and final. Every matchup links to fighter profiles. Expand buttons expose additional fight metadata.

## Strengths
- Best direct model for JKBF’s championship information architecture.
- Clear separation of title fights and tournament structure.
- Round tabs reduce long bracket content while keeping the hierarchy visible.
- Ticket action remains close to the event proposition.

## Weaknesses / adaptation risk
- Dense event pages can become difficult to scan on small screens.
- International tournament labels should be translated to JKBF’s audience and rules.

## JKBF adaptation
Use GLORY as the primary reference for Championship, Fighters, Fighter Details and Bracket. Retain the visual identity of JKBF and use actual athlete portraits; do not copy GLORY’s logo treatment or event art. Priority P0 for championship and bracket, complexity Medium–High.
