# Gymshark — feature analysis

**Observed URL:** `https://www.gymshark.com/`.

## Homepage and commerce patterns
Gymshark uses a utility bar, Women/Men/Accessories/Explore navigation, search, wishlist, account and cart actions. The homepage opens with a campaign hero with video controls and two CTAs, followed by featured collections, product carousels, audience tabs, training categories, guides, help/account/footer links, payment marks and social links.

## Strengths
- Strong progression from campaign storytelling to collections to products.
- Clear product metadata and price in carousel cards.
- Wishlist, account and cart are persistently discoverable.
- Trust cues include shipping, payment methods, returns and help.

## Limitations
The collection routes tested in this session returned 404; product-detail and mobile interactions were not persisted.

## JKBF adaptation
Use the campaign-to-product flow, collection grouping, product card metadata and trust footer for Merch and Product Details. Avoid cloning a fashion-commerce megamenu; JKBF needs a compact merch store. Priority P1, complexity Medium.
