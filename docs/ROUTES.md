# Application Routes

## Public Routes
- `/`: Homepage
- `/tentang`: About the event
- `/jadwal`: Event Schedule
- `/lineup`: Concert & Entertainment lineup
- `/venue`: Venue & Transport info
- `/fighters`: Fighter Directory
- `/fighters/[slug]`: Fighter Profile
- `/championship`: Championship Overview
- `/bracket`: Tournament Brackets
- `/city-pride`: City Pride Voting Engine
- `/merch`: Merch Store
- `/merch/[slug]`: Merch Product Page
- `/cart`: Shopping Cart
- `/checkout`: Checkout Flow
- `/orders`: Order History & Tracking
- `/creator`: Creator Hub Landing
- `/creator/login`: Creator Login
- `/creator/register`: Creator Registration
- `/creator/dashboard`: Creator Dashboard
- `/ppv`: Pay-Per-View Landing Page
- `/ppv/watch`: Secure PPV Streaming Page
- `/faq`: Frequently Asked Questions
- `/kontak`: Official Contact
- `/kebijakan-privasi`: Privacy Policy
- `/syarat-ketentuan`: Terms & Conditions

## Admin Routes
- `/admin`: Command Center Dashboard
- `/admin/cms`: Content Management
- `/admin/ecommerce`: Orders & Products Management
- `/admin/championship`: Matches & Athletes Management
- `/admin/creators`: Creator Submissions & Points
- `/admin/voting`: City Pride Management
- `/admin/users`: User & Role Management
- `/admin/settings`: System Settings
